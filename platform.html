Our Platform

These aren’t just talking points. They’re the ingredients that make the Donut Party who we are.

🍩

Personal Freedom

Every American should be free to choose their own glaze, filling, and toppings. No central kitchen deciding for you.

💪

Strong Economy

We believe in rising dough and rising wages. More bakeries, more jobs, less government flour taxes.

🛡️

Secure Borders

A good donut shop has a door. We believe in controlled entry — not an open kitchen for every recipe under the sun.

🇺🇸

American-Made

From the flour to the frosting, we prefer made-in-America donuts over imported, overprocessed rice policies.

⚖️

Limited Government

There’s a reason donuts have a hole. Government shouldn’t try to fill every space in your life.

🔥

Energy & Growth

We support policies that keep the ovens hot and the economy rising — not stuck in a sticky mess.
